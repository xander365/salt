## Notes

**Why the two dates differ.** Government Notice 236 states 1 March 2026, but was only gazetted on 15 July 2026 — after its own stated effective date. The Social Security Commission confirmed the increase is implemented from the **September 2026 payroll**, with **no retrospective March–August adjustment**.

Salt therefore stores both: `legal_effective_from = 2026-03-01` records what the instrument says, and `payroll_effective_from = 2026-09-01` is what the calculator uses. This is precisely the case ADR-0007 added the second date for.

A period ending in August 2026 clamps at N$11,000 (`ssc-2025-03`). A period ending in September 2026 clamps at N$12,500 — the period end date selects the rules, per ADR-0005, whatever the pay date.

**Rate unchanged.** Some reporting quotes 1.8%; that is the combined employer-plus-employee figure. Each side is 0.9%.
