# ssc-2025-03

**Kind:** SSC rules
**legal_effective_from:** 2025-03-01
**payroll_effective_from:** 2025-03-01
**Superseded by:** `ssc-2026-09` from the September 2026 payroll
**Statutory cases:** SC-SSC-001, SC-SSC-002, SC-SSC-007

## Values

```text
employee rate:        0.9%
employer rate:        0.9%
minimum basic wage:   N$500/month    ->  N$4.50 per side
maximum basic wage:   N$11,000/month ->  N$99.00 per side
```

Base: `BasicPay` only — the statutory `basic wage`, excluding allowances, overtime, additional Sunday, public-holiday and night-work pay, and pension, annuity, medical and insurance benefits.

## Notes

This is the ceiling in force through the **August 2026** payroll. The N$12,500 increase was gazetted with a stated date of 1 March 2026 but deferred in practice — see `ssc-2026-09.md`.

**Conflicting minimum.** The US SSA country profile (2019) gives the minimum earnings base as N$300, not N$500. Salt implements N$500: it is what current professional reporting, the shipped code, and the published N$4.50 minimum contribution all give. Recorded as SC-OPEN-5.
