# paye-2024-03

**Kind:** PAYE table
**legal_effective_from:** 2024-03-01
**payroll_effective_from:** 2024-03-01
**Statutory cases:** SC-PAYE-001 … SC-PAYE-013 (see `docs/domain/statutory-conformance.md` §7)
**Status:** current for the 2026/27 year of assessment

## Values

| Annual taxable income | Tax |
|---|---:|
| N$0 – N$100,000 | No tax |
| N$100,001 – N$150,000 | 18% of the amount above N$100,000 |
| N$150,001 – N$350,000 | N$9,000 + 25% of the amount above N$150,000 |
| N$350,001 – N$550,000 | N$59,000 + 28% of the amount above N$350,000 |
| N$550,001 – N$850,000 | N$115,000 + 30% of the amount above N$550,000 |
| N$850,001 – N$1,550,000 | N$205,000 + 32% of the amount above N$850,000 |
| Above N$1,550,000 | N$429,000 + 37% of the amount above N$1,550,000 |

## Notes

**Brochure typo.** The NamRA brochure prints the sixth row as *"Exceeds N$ 800 000 but does not exceed N$ 1 550 000"*. Every other source gives **N$ 850,000**, and the table's own arithmetic requires it: `115,000 + 30% × 300,000 = 205,000` only holds if the band runs 550,000 → 850,000. Salt implements N$850,000.

**Internal consistency verified.** Each band's base amount equals the cumulative tax at the boundary below it:

```text
 18% × 50,000            =   9,000
  9,000 + 25% × 200,000  =  59,000
 59,000 + 28% × 200,000  = 115,000
115,000 + 30% × 300,000  = 205,000
205,000 + 32% × 700,000  = 429,000
```

**Not covered by this table.** How one payroll period's PAYE is derived from these annual bands is not prescribed by any source found — see `docs/domain/statutory-conformance.md` SC-OPEN-1. Rounding is likewise unprescribed (SC-OPEN-2). Those are Salt policy and are tested as `salt_policy_*`.

**Deductions.** Approved pension, provident fund, retirement annuity and child-education policy premiums reduce taxable income, capped at N$150,000 combined per tax year. Salt v1 supports none of them and refuses an employee who has one.
