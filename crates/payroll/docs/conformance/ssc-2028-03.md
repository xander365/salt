## Notes

Scheduled in the same Government Notice as the N$12,500 increase. Unlike that one, this step's gazetted date and its payroll application date agree — the deferral applied only to the first increase, which was gazetted after its own stated date (see `ssc-2026-09.md`).

Ships ahead of its effective date because the whole schedule is already published. If the SSC defers this step as it deferred the first, `payroll_effective_from` moves and `legal_effective_from` does not.
